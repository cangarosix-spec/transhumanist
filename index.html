About the Book

For those who refuse to age by the old rules

We are living through a moment future historians will describe as the most important transitional point in human history. Technologies once dismissed as fiction — gene editing, neural implants, AI that surpasses human ability — are becoming reality in real time.

TRANSHUMANIST is not sci-fi speculation. Every fact, every claim, is grounded in research happening right now — at Harvard, in Silicon Valley, in Beijing.

The book is written so that anyone who wants to be on the right side of change can understand it — no PhD, no technical background required. Just curiosity and the courage to look at the future with open eyes.

Author
Joe Lann
Writer · Researcher · Transhumanist

Joe Lann writes at the intersection of science, philosophy, and the future. For years he has followed the latest research in longevity, neuroscience, and artificial intelligence — translating it into language anyone can understand and apply.

He believes we are the first generation within reach of biological immortality — and that it's our responsibility to understand what that means before it happens.

TRANSHUMANIST is the result of years of research, conversations with some of the sharpest minds in the field, and personal experience with biohacking and optimization.
